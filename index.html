About the Editor

A creative mind. An editorial perspective. An entrepreneurial vision.

Anik Adhikary is a multidisciplinary creative professional, graphic designer, journalist and entrepreneur based in Jashore, Bangladesh.

He has 5 years of experience in graphic design and 2 years of experience in journalism.

His work spans visual communication, editorial design, newspaper layout, advertising, fashion design, news publishing and independent entrepreneurship.

He combines creative thinking with journalistic practice and develops independent ventures across media, technology, fashion and creative commerce.

He is the founder of DAINIK ANULIPI, OPTIQO WEB, DREADSTAR and GRAPHIC DESIGN OCEAN.
